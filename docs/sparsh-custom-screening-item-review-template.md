## Approval rule

An item may enter an approved manifest only after age applicability, domain, wording, administration, source/evidence, rights, and required reviews are resolved and documented. Form-level approval must also cover the age boundaries, intended use, and safety/follow-up plan. Clinical item approval does not establish validated scoring or diagnostic validity. Retain rejected and superseded records for audit, but exclude them from the active manifest.
