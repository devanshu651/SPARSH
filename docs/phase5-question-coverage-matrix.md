# Phase 5 question coverage audit and source-backed expansion proposal

**Status:** audit/proposal only. No production content, selector, scoring, frontend or deployment changes were made. Active data audited: `phase5-final-65-v2` (`backend/app/config/milestones.json`). Source-derived age listings are evidence metadata; they are not clinical cutoffs or validation.

## Executive summary

- The active dataset contains 65 unique records (13 per domain): 36 CDC source-checkpoint records, 26 AAP surveillance mean-age records marked `review_required`, and 3 WHO attainment-range records marked `range`. All 65 source references say reviewed, but all clinical and wording review statuses are pending.
- Per the current strict eligibility function, eligible item counts equal the active source-checkpoint counts except held records. It selects only at the exact lower checkpoint chosen for chronological age; it does not carry a prior question forward. AAP means and WHO attainment windows remain ineligible absent an approved applicability policy.
- No checkpoint approaches the requested 8 items/domain. Gross and fine motor each have zero currently eligible items at every checkpoint. The other three domains range from zero to three.
- The 95-record `milestones_candidate_v2.json` is a source-backed candidate pool, not approved production content: it overlaps 48 original IDs and contains 47 added AAP/WHO motor candidates. Thirty of its records are absent from the active 65; nine of those are additional CDC records, nineteen AAP mean-age records, and two WHO range records. Their statuses remain pending.
- The legacy 155 is not safe to reactivate wholesale. The documented 95-record pool accounts for 48 legacy IDs; the remaining 107 have no item-level source provenance in that legacy file/candidate pool and must remain excluded pending evidence and review.
- At current strict eligibility, the missing count to the minimum target of 8/domain is exactly 8 in GM and FM at every active checkpoint; for other domains it is 5?8 based on current counts. The evidence pool does not yet demonstrate age-range applicability sufficient to fix this.

## Counting policy

Checkpoint counts below reproduce the active service rule: (1) floor completed chronological age to the greatest active checkpoint not above it; (2) include reviewed-source `checkpoint` records only when checkpoint exactly matches; (3) exclude `review_required`, `range` without an approved screening-applicability basis, and review-held content. CDC milestone ages are monitoring source listings and not SPARSH clinical cutoffs. ?Eligible checkpoints? in the item audit means eligible under that current strict rule, not merely source age nearby. For ages between listed checkpoints, selector uses the preceding checkpoint. The configured age list also includes 30 and 72 months; the active catalog has no approved source checkpoint there.

## Proposed active catalog and selection logic

- **Do not change the active catalog yet.** The currently supportable proposed pool is the 95-record candidate set strictly as a non-production review queue, comprising 48 carried-forward source records and 47 new AAP/WHO motor concepts. The 95 count is not a proposed production approval and does not reach target counts under current rules.
- Of the 95, all remain clinical/wording pending; WHO records need exact range handling and reuse review; AAP mean ages must never be auto-converted to checkpoints. Existing held items (`co_12_01`, `co_36_01`, `co_36_03`) stay excluded pending their documented review decisions.
- Keep exact source checkpoint selection as-is for reviewed approved checkpoint items. If future policy approves ranges, eligibility must require explicitly reviewed ?screening applicability? plus observed range containment. Do not rank randomly or add clinical importance weights.
- Deterministic selection rule if future approved content exceeds nine in a domain: filter for approved domain + age applicability; sort by exact source checkpoint before approved range; then exact age proximity to selected checkpoint; then stronger documented evidence status; then stable ID as final tie-break. This is only a technical ordering proposal, not a clinical importance model. Existing production set is below nine, so no truncation/ranking applies now.

## Minimum additional source-backed content required

At every currently active checkpoint, currently eligible counts are 0 GM, 0 FM, 0?3 Language, 0?3 Cognitive, and 0?2 Social-Emotional. Reaching a minimum 8 in each domain requires the per-cell missing counts in the checkpoint matrix (32?40 additional eligible concepts per age-set in total, before resolving overlap and review). A shared content bank may reuse a question across multiple checkpoints only after a source-supported age range and professional screening-applicability policy explicitly support that use. Current evidence does not establish those ranges for the CDC or AAP records. Target 8?9 is therefore unmet and cannot be responsibly claimed from this repository today.
