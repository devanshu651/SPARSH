## Motor coverage and unresolved additions

- **Gross Motor:** 0 strict eligible questions at all active checkpoints. The expansion pool includes AAP-age concepts plus six WHO gross-motor attainment-window concepts. AAP mean ages are not screening cutoffs; WHO windows are not screening applicability. CDC?s combined Movement/Physical Development category does not establish the gross/fine split for legacy records. No source-backed motor additions currently close checkpoint gaps under the strict selector.
- **Fine Motor:** 0 strict eligible questions at all active checkpoints. AAP motor table gives fine-motor observations and mean ages, but age assignment remains review-required. The 107 recovery pass finds CDC combined-movement items only partially supportive for the domain split; they do not become eligible Fine Motor questions automatically.
- Candidate counts per domain in the 168-item pool are: Gross Motor 46, Fine Motor 49, Language 26, Cognitive 22, Social-Emotional 25. These are content-bank counts, not checkpoint coverage.

## Selection/provenance interpretation

Counts use the same eligibility predicate as the current typed selector: checkpoint type, reviewed source reference, source checkpoint exactly equal to selected lower checkpoint, and no explicit exclusion status. `review_required` and `range` records are excluded. Direct CDC candidate records are age-listed but still pending clinical/wording review; numbers are coverage diagnostics for candidate review, not permission to activate them. No arbitrary age ranges or ranking weights were introduced.
